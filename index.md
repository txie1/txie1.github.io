## hi there! 👋

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in [Computer Science at UCLA](https://www.cs.ucla.edu/), fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Cho-Jui Hsieh](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~chohsieh/).

I am broadly interested in post-training of large language models. Currently, I am working on supervised fine-tuning (SFT), reinforcement learning (RL), and reward modeling, to improve reasoning capabilities and encourage stronger generalization.

Feel free to connect with me and explore opportunities, collaborations, and exciting ventures together!
