## fun & cool things

Beyond academic pursuits, I am also actively involved in student organizations at UCLA. In particular, [Global Entrepreneurship Society (GES) at UCLA](https://gesatucla.org/) is a great place that ignites curiosity for new innovations and encourages entrepreneurial thinking. I also had the opportunity to work with [Startup UCLA](https://startupucla.com/), where the dynamic startup ecosystem gave me hands-on experience of turning groundbreaking ideas into reality.
